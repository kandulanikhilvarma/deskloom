# Deskloom

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user. Next.js and TypeScript support a responsive editor and private server routes. Neon supplies Postgres and managed authentication. Vercel hosts the app.

## Users
People who need a productive desk in an apartment or home office. They want to reuse existing equipment and spend within a fixed budget.

## Product Purpose
Turn a space photo and personal constraints into useful desk plans with real product links.

## Operating Context
Users capture a photo on a phone or upload one on a computer. They compare alternatives, retain owned equipment, swap products, and save a plan.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The first release has three design directions, budget checks, region-specific products, interactive layouts, private saved plans, and an export.
India is the assumed first market. US and Germany catalogs are separate. Euro prices describe Germany, not every EU country.
Photorealistic AI transformations need an activated image provider. A schematic preview must not imply exact product appearance or dimensions.
No paid commitment, legal acceptance, affiliate claim, stock claim, or medical claim is authorized.

## Evidence on Hand
Official product pages and workstation guidance will support the catalog and advice. There are no customer metrics or testimonials.

## Product Principles
Keep owned equipment before any purchase.
Keep the total within the selected budget.
Show source links and price uncertainty.
Keep personal photos private.

## Accessibility and Inclusion
Support keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, and phone touch controls.
